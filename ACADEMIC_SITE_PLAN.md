# Academic Site Plan

## Purpose

This repository should serve as the public academic homepage for Hollis36. The site should present a coherent research identity around multimodal perception, industrial embodied intelligence, robotics / VLA, smart manufacturing, and AI for scientific or industrial systems.

## Public Positioning

Suggested positioning statement:

> I work on multimodal perception, industrial embodied intelligence, and robot learning, with a focus on robust visual sensing, vision-language-action models, smart manufacturing, and deployable AI systems for scientific and industrial applications.

## Research Identity

The site should emphasize four coherent research lines:

### 1. Multimodal Perception

RGB-T / RGB-D fusion, robust object detection, small-object detection, and multimodal representation learning.

### 2. Industrial Embodied Intelligence

Robot perception, planning, manipulation, industrial execution, calibration, and deployable embodied AI systems.

### 3. UAV and Anti-UAV Intelligence

Low-altitude sensing, drone detection, UAV scene understanding, RGB-T anti-UAV detection, and VLM-assisted target verification.

### 4. Smart Manufacturing and AI for Engineering Systems

Process optimization, defect generation, dynamic monitoring, error compensation, digital twins, and robot-assisted manufacturing.

## Design Principles

- Use a clean academic layout with a white or light background
- Keep the homepage concise and project-oriented
- Prefer high-quality figures over long text blocks
- Use consistent project cards with title, one-sentence contribution, image, and links
- Separate polished representative work from exploratory repositories
- Make the site readable for both academic reviewers and industry recruiters
