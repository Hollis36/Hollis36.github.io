Embodied AI × Industrial Automation

Foundation models are moving from screens into the physical world, yet factories still need outputs that are correct, verifiable and able to run on the hardware they already have. My research sits where embodied AI meets industrial automation, and I build my methods as working systems that run offline on factory PCs, CPU-only laptops and robot controllers.

GitHub

[Research]

  1. 01Foundation Models for Robot Programming & Inspection

    Turning language and vision-language models into robot programs and inspection decisions that can be verified: in simulation before a robot moves, and on held-out data before a model is deployed.

  2. 02Robust Multimodal Perception

    Perceiving small, low-contrast targets by fusing RGB and thermal imagery, and by distilling what large vision-language models know into detectors fast enough for real time.

  3. 03Physics-Informed AI for Manufacturing

    Combining multi-sensor data with physical models to monitor additive manufacturing and other high-value production processes online.
