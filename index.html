Peifu Zhang

Researcher in Robot Learning & Intelligent Manufacturing

State Key Laboratory of Electromechanical Integrated Manufacturing of High-performance Electronic Equipment, School of Electro-Mechanical Engineering, Xidian University

Goal: Building grounded AI that perceives, reasons, and acts reliably in the physical world.

Research Interests: Vision-language models and LLMs for robots and manufacturing: program generation with simulation verification, RL post-training and evaluation of VLMs, industrial visual inspection, and RGB–infrared perception.

[Research at a Glance]

  1. 01
    Multimodal Perception & Fusion

    Fusing complementary signals (vision and language, optical and X-ray imaging) so models perceive more reliably than any single modality allows.

  2. 02
    AI for Intelligent Manufacturing

    Fusing multi-source sensing with physical knowledge to monitor and automate additive and integrated manufacturing of high-performance electronic equipment.

  3. 03
    VLM/LLM for Robot Programming & Industrial Inspection

    Using vision-language models and LLMs to program robots and inspect industrial parts, with verification in simulation and careful evaluation of what post-training really gains.
