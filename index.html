Peifu Zhang

Ph.D. student in Mechanical Engineering, Xidian University

I work on AI that lets robots see, understand instructions and get work done, mostly in factories.

Most factory robots are still programmed line by line by engineers, and parts are often inspected by fixed rules. I want vision and language models to take on part of this work: see hard-to-spot targets, understand what people ask for, and produce programs that run on real machines, with mistakes caught before they happen.

[What I Work On]

  1. 01

    Seeing more reliably

    Helping machines spot small targets in poor light, for example finding a distant drone by combining visible and infrared images.

  2. 02

    Smarter manufacturing

    Combining sensor data with physics to catch faults while metal parts are being 3D printed.

  3. 03

    Language models that run robots

    Using large models to write robot programs and inspect industrial parts, checked in simulation before they touch a real machine.
